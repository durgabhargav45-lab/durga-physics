    Reflection: light bouncing back

    Two laws explain every mirror you will meet, flat or curved. Test them with your own hands.

    Law 1

    Angle of incidence = angle of reflection. Both angles are measured from the normal (the dashed line at 90° to the mirror), never from the mirror surface.

    Law 2

    The incident ray, the normal and the reflected ray all lie in the same plane.

    Refraction: why light bends

    Light moves at different speeds in different materials. When a beam enters a new material at a slant, one side slows (or speeds up) first, so the whole beam turns.

    Activity 9.7: why does the coin look raised?

    Your eye follows the bent ray backwards in a straight line. That is why the coin seems to float up.

    Glass slab: in, bend, out, shifted

    A ray bends towards the normal on entering glass and away from it on leaving. Because the two faces are parallel, the ray comes out parallel to where it started, just shifted sideways.
